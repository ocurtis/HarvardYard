GAHD THE YAHD

A tower defense in Harvard Yard, with bells.

▶ PLAY

You are the ghost of John Harvard: keep the drunk frat bros from pissing on your statue through fifteen rounds, and pay for it by shepherding lost tourists to their photos.

Controls

WASD / arrowsMove.
J / X / ShiftHold to deliver a sermon. Sobers and stops bros in a cone in front of you. Also scares tourists, so mind your aim.
Walk into a guideHand a lost tourist group a brochure. They pay when they reach their photo spot. Leave them confused and they storm out and cost you.
SpaceStart the next round. Rounds never start on their own.
1 / 2Build a Dig ($30) or a Sprinkler ($20) on the grass you're standing on, any time.
UUpgrade the turret you're standing on.
3 / 4 / 5Upgrade your sermon's power, its range, or your speed.
MMute.

Enemies

BroWalks the whole path to the statue, unzips, lets go. Turrets and sermons both work. Every round.
LongshotFast, with a beer funnel. Stops the moment the statue is in range and arcs it over everything. Shakes because he has to go. From round 3.
Cup ChuckerFat, slow, stops near the gate and lobs full cups at the statue. Turrets can't touch him. Catch a cup by running under its shadow, or sermon it out of the air. From round 5.
Finals Club MemberTuxedo, champagne, nearly your speed, and takes a lot of sobering. Walks straight in mid-round. From round 8.

Every bro gets 6% drunker each round. Sobering one out pays $1.

Turrets

Dig · $30An archaeological dig that lobs bones at the nearest bro in range. Upgrade for range, rate and punch.
Sprinkler · $20Soaks everyone in a small circle: bros slow down and sober a little; tourists just walk slower for a moment.

Turrets never hurt tourists. Only your sermon can scare money away.

Tourists

Tour groupsArrive in threes to fives behind a guide and stop, confused, a few tiles in. Their value ticks down while they wait. Touch the guide to send them to the statue ($10 a head) or a building ($7 a head).
The statue100 HP. When it's gone, so are you.
