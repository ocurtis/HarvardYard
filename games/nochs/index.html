NOCH'S RUSH

▶ PLAY ◀ GAME SELECT

You're on the counter at Pinocchio's during a rush. The cutter keeps sliding Sicilian squares at you; four open boxes each have an order card. Pack every box exactly the way the card shows, before the conveyor backs up.

Controls

1 2 3 4Pick a box.
WASD / arrowsPick the cell in that box.
J / X / SpaceDrop the square in your hand into the cell. No undo: the lid closes on mistakes.
RPut the square on the day-old rack instead. Three slots; a fourth is a strike.

Scoring

CleanAll four cells as ordered. $3, and a bonus every third clean box in a row.
MessyOne cell wrong. $1, and the streak resets.
Sent backTwo or more wrong. No tip, one strike.
FloorA square arriving on a full conveyor hits the floor. One strike.

Three strikes ends the shift. Survive bar close for a clean shift. A grey cell with a dot on an order card means "any square".

Trying the other way to pack

Add ?placement=box to the play URL and squares fill the chosen box in reading order; you only pick the box. ?placement=cells is the default.
