# Reference board

Photos are for costume, pose, color and setting only. Never draw a likeness of anyone in them.

- `rock_on_island.png` — Rock On! Island (UFO 50). The style contract: 16px tiles, 32-color palette,
  big-head proportions (head ~40% of a 16px sprite), selective dark outlines, flat fills with one
  shade step, dense tower placement, left HUD column, winding single-width yellow path.
- `bros_group.png` — the bro. Harvard bro, not frat-movie bro: untucked light button-down (white,
  light blue, pink), loosened tie, khakis, boat shoes or sneakers, hair swoop. Drunk version: tie
  worn on the head or undone, shirttails out, red cup. Group stance: arms over shoulders, leaning.
- `statue_tourists.png` — two things. (1) The tourist: navy blazer, khakis, rep tie (red/navy
  stripes), buttoned up, posed for a photo. Neat where the bro is dishevelled. (2) The statue and its
  setting: seated bronze figure (dark green-brown), hands on knees, on a tall rose-granite plinth with
  the JOHN HARVARD plaque; the toe of the left shoe rubbed bright gold. Behind it University Hall:
  grey granite blocks, tall multi-pane windows, white portico. The bottom wall of the map is this.

Palette hints (from the UFO 50 32): bronze #783450/#003d10 with #8a6042 highlight; plinth #a48080;
granite #c8c8c8 + #707070; brick #991515 + #ca4a00; khaki #feb854 (light) / #8a6042 (shadow);
navy blazer #202040 with #006ab4 edge; shirts #ffffff, #27badb, #fe626e; tie #e03c32; the gold toe #e8ea4a.
