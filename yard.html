GAHD THE YAHD

▶ PLAY ◀ GAME SELECT

You are the ghost of John Harvard: keep the drunk frat bros from pissing on your statue through fifteen rounds, and pay for it by shepherding lost tourists to their photos.

Controls

WASD / arrowsMove.
J / X / ShiftHold to deliver a sermon. Bros in the cone in front of you slow down, stop what they're doing and sober up. It also scares tourists, so mind your aim.
Walk into a tour guideHands the group a brochure. They pay when they reach their photo spot. Leave them confused too long and they storm out and cost you $10.
Space / EnterStart the next round. Rounds never start on their own.
1 / 2 / 3Build a Dig ($30), a Sprinkler ($20) or the Amp ($45) on the grass you're standing on, any time. The Amp faces the way you're facing.
UUpgrade the tower you're standing on.
4 / 5 / 6Upgrade your sermon's power, its range, or your speed.

Enemies

BroWalks the whole path to the statue, unzips, lets go. Towers and sermons both work. Every round.
LongshotFast, with a beer funnel. Stops the moment the statue is in range and arcs it over everything in between. He shakes the whole way in because he has to go. From round 3.
Cup ChuckerSlow. Waddles a few tiles in, stops, and lobs full cups at the statue every five seconds. Towers can't touch him. Catch a cup by running under its shadow, or knock it down with a sermon. From round 5.
Finals Club MemberTuxedo, champagne, almost as fast as you, and takes nearly three bros' worth of sobering. Walks straight in mid-round. From round 8.

Every round, every bro arrives 6% drunker than the round before. Sobering one out pays $1, whoever he is.

Towers

Dig · $30An archaeological dig site that lobs bones at the nearest bro in range. One upgrade: more range, faster bones, harder bones.
Sprinkler · $20Soaks everyone in a small circle: bros slow down and sober a little; tourists just walk slower for a moment.
The Amp · $45A half-stack and the man playing through it. Point it down a lane: the wind blows bros back the way they came, and new arrivals route around it and take the other way in. It can't wall the Yard off (the game won't let you build one that would), bros already past it don't turn around, and chuckers are too heavy to blow. Upgrade: longer, wider wind.

Towers never hurt tourists. Only your sermon can scare money away.

Tourists

Tour groupsArrive in threes to fives behind a guide and stop, confused, a few tiles in. Their value ticks down while they wait. Touch the guide to lock in what they're still worth and send them on their way. Groups headed for the statue pay $10 a head; groups headed for a building pay $7.
The statue100 HP. When it's gone, so are you.
