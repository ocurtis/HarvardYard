Yard Voice Booth

Record a take, name it, Save & push. It lands in the repo on GitHub; Claude pulls it and chip-crushes it.

Allow the microphone when asked. Each take: one sound, under a second or two.
  1. shout: the player yelling at a bro ("HEY!")
  2. ugh: a bro sobering up and giving up
  3. woo: a frat "wooo" for the wind-up
  4. splat: mouth splat for the cup landing
  5. anything else you think belongs
